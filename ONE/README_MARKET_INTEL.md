# Market Intelligence

Operational workspace for market research and movement tracking.

## Folders

- `studies`: deep-dive reports and one-off analysis.
- `movements`: short tactical notes about market moves.
- `templates`: standard templates for repeatable quality.

## Weekly Cadence

1. Add raw findings to `movements`.
2. Promote high-signal findings into `studies`.
3. Convert validated findings into roadmap actions inside product planning.
